About the Astrologer

Predictions people remember, year after year

Venu Swamy is widely regarded as Telangana's leading celebrity astrologer — the name film stars, politicians, business leaders, and thousands of families turn to before major life decisions. Trained in traditional Jyotish shastra under respected gurus, he has spent over three decades reading horoscopes with a level of accuracy that has built his reputation almost entirely on word of mouth.

Clients return to him again and again because his predictions have a habit of coming true — from career turning points to marriage timing to business outcomes. His approach blends classical shastric method — planetary positions, dashas, and nakshatras — with clear, direct guidance rather than vague reassurance. He remains a recurring guest on regional television and radio, and continues to conduct pujas and remedial ceremonies at his consultation chamber.
